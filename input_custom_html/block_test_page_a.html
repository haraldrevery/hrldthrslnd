When to use this one

Variant A is the default, and what index.html leads with. It works with no imagery at all, which makes it the safe choice for an essay, an index page, or anything where the photographs come later in the page rather than at the top. It is the one hero that answers to light and dark mode; switch your system setting with this page open and the stage, the type and the grid all move with it.

Compare it with variant B, which leads with a photograph instead.
