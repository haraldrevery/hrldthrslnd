Choosing between the two

Variant B needs one photograph strong enough to carry a whole screen, and quiet enough in the top third that a headline can sit on it. Where there is no such picture, variant A is the better answer — it asks for no imagery at all.

Both are the same block underneath. Swapping one for the other is adding or removing .hero-stage-photo and the .hero-media layer; the height, the bottom fade, the padding that keeps the copy clear of that fade, and the type colours are all in .hero-stage and do not change.

What to check before shipping one

Look at it in both colour schemes, on a phone in landscape, and with the picture replaced by a much brighter one. The first two are where the layout gives, the third is where the scrim does.
