## Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and makes no third-party requests.
Nothing you do here is recorded by this site. Your host's server logs are the
only record of a visit, and they are outside the scope of this page.

Two features store a small amount of data in your own browser and send it
nowhere: the reading-width control remembers your chosen column width in
`localStorage`. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.
